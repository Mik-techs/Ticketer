Privacy Policy

Last updated: check with the shop for the current date this was published.

This page explains, plainly, what happens to your information when you place an order through our website. It describes what this specific system actually does - not a generic template.

What we collect

When you place an order, we collect:

  • Your name
  • What you want printed, and the details of that job (copies, color, size, finishing, or the equivalent details for the product type - e.g. shirt size for T-shirts)
  • A file you upload, if you choose to attach one, plus a generated image of your order ticket

We do not ask for your phone number or email directly. If you send your order to us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp shares your number with us as part of how WhatsApp itself works - that happens between you and WhatsApp, governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy, not something this website controls.

How it's used

Your order details are sent to the shop as a WhatsApp message, and separately saved to a database so staff can see, organize, and track orders over time. That's the whole purpose of collecting it - fulfilling your print order and keeping a record of it.

Where it's stored

We use two outside services to run this system:

  • Supabase - hosts the order database. Only the shop's logged-in staff can view or manage orders stored there.
  • Catbox.moe - a free file-hosting service used to hold any file you upload and the generated order-ticket image, so a link to it can be included in your order.
Worth knowing: files uploaded through Catbox get a private-feeling but not truly access-controlled link - nobody can find it by searching, but anyone who has the exact link could open it. Please don't upload anything highly sensitive (like a passport photo page) if that link ever being seen by someone outside the shop would concern you. If in doubt, choose "I'll bring it myself" instead of uploading.

Your choices

  • Uploading a file is always optional - you can choose to bring your item in person instead.
  • You can ask the shop to delete your order record at any time using the contact details below.

Cookies

This site doesn't use cookies, and doesn't use any advertising or analytics trackers. The only thing stored in your browser is a login session - and only on the staff dashboard, not on the customer order form or this page - so staff don't have to log in again every time they open it.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept in the database until the shop removes them. There's currently no automatic deletion after a set time.

A note on the law

This page describes our actual practices but isn't legal advice. Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) governs how personal data must be handled by businesses in Ghana, and may require registering with the Data Protection Commission depending on the nature and scale of data processing. The shop owner should confirm their specific obligations with the Commission or a local advisor rather than relying on this page alone.
