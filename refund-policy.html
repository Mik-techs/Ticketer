Refund Policy

This is a starting draft, not your finalized policy. Custom/personalized print work is commonly non-refundable once produced - that's reflected below as a reasonable default - but every CONFIRM marker is a real decision only the shop owner can make. Please review before publishing.

Made-to-order printing

Because most of what we print is made specifically for you - business cards with your details, banners with your design, T-shirts with your print - orders generally can't be refunded once production has started, the same way a custom order works at most print shops.

When a reprint or refund may apply

CONFIRM - we'll typically offer a free reprint or refund if:

  • The finished item doesn't match what was confirmed with you (wrong quantity, wrong color mode, wrong size, etc. - caused by our error, not a mistake in what was submitted)
  • There's a genuine quality/print defect

What doesn't qualify

CONFIRM - typically not covered:

  • Changing your mind after the order was confirmed and printing began
  • Errors in the file, design, spelling, or details you submitted yourself
  • Minor, expected variation in color between a screen and a printed result
